In the app

  1. Open ClothMarket and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password and confirm.

If you cannot use the app

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from your registered contact with the subject "Delete my ClothMarket account", stating the mobile number used for the account. We will verify ownership and delete the account.

What is deleted

Your profile, posts, chat and voice messages (sent and received), blocks, reports you filed, and your uploaded photos, videos and voice files. Reports other users filed about your account keep only a numeric account identifier for safety and legal purposes. Backups are overwritten on the normal backup cycle.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
