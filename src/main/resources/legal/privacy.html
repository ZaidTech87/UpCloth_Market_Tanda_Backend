ClothMarket ("the app") is operated by {{COMPANY_NAME}}, {{POSTAL_ADDRESS}} ("we"). This policy explains what personal data the app handles and why. The app is for people aged 18 or older.

Data we collect

  • Account data you give us: name, location (as you type it), email address, mobile number, and a password (stored only as a one-way hash).
  • Content you create: profile photo, listing posts (description, price, quantity, cloth type, photo or video), chat messages and voice messages.
  • Safety data: reports you file, accounts you block, and the date and version of the Terms and Privacy Policy you accepted.
  • Technical data: your IP address is processed to apply rate limits and protect against abuse. Audio and video calls are sent directly between devices (WebRTC); if a direct path is not possible they pass through a relay server and are not recorded or stored by us. Call signalling data is not stored.

How we use it

To create and secure your account, verify your email address and let you reset your password by sending one-time passwords (OTPs) to your email, show your listings and profile to other users, deliver messages and calls, review reported or unsafe content, prevent abuse and fraud, and meet legal obligations. One-time passwords are short-lived, can be used once, and are kept only in a hashed form while they are valid.

Who can see what

Your name, location, profile photo and listings are visible to other users. Your mobile number is not shown to other users. Messages are visible to you and the person you message.

Service providers who process data for us

  • Cloud hosting and database provider (stores all account data)
  • Cloudflare R2 (stores uploaded photos, videos and voice messages in private storage)
  • Resend (delivers one-time password emails to your email address)
  • Sightengine (automatically checks uploaded images for unsafe content)
  • Groq (generates replies for the in-app assistant; text you type into the assistant is sent to it)
  • A TURN relay provider, if configured, to relay calls between devices

We do not sell your personal data and the app shows no third-party advertising.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account) or by following the deletion instructions. Deletion removes your profile, posts, messages (sent and received), blocks, reports you filed, and your uploaded files. Reports that other users filed about your account keep only a numeric account identifier, for safety and legal purposes. Backups are overwritten on the normal backup cycle.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. Contact us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

Security

We use encrypted connections, hashed passwords, access controls and rate limiting. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will notify affected users and the authorities as the law requires.
