3. Your consent, and how to withdraw it

We process your personal data with your consent, which you give when you create your account and accept the Terms and this policy, and when you allow the app to use your camera or microphone. The Act also allows some processing without consent in limited cases (for example to meet a legal obligation, to respond to a medical or safety emergency, or where you have voluntarily given us data for a purpose and have not objected). We use that only where the law allows it.

You can withdraw consent at any time, and it is as easy as giving it:

  • Everything: delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or follow the deletion instructions. We cannot provide an account without the data it needs, so withdrawing consent for that data means the account is closed.
  • Camera or microphone: turn the permission off in your phone's settings. Calls, voice messages or taking photos will then not work.
  • AI assistant: stop using it. We then receive nothing further for it.

Withdrawing consent does not affect what we did lawfully before you withdrew.

4. Who can see your data

  • Other users can see your name, location, profile photo and listings. Your mobile number and email address are not shown to other users.
  • Chat and voice messages are visible to you and the person you message. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. We do not read messages as a routine matter. We may look at a message that someone reports, where needed to act on a report or where the law requires.
  • Our administrators can see account details (name, email, mobile number, location, profile photo, registration date) and listings to run the service, handle reports and enforce the Terms. They can edit these details on request or to correct errors, block an account, and delete accounts and listings. Each such action is recorded.
  • Government and courts, where the law requires us to disclose information.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we process about you, what processing we do, and the other parties we share it with;
  • Correct, complete or update your personal data (you can change much of it in the app, or ask us);
  • Erase your personal data (see section 8), unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • Grievance redressal: raise a complaint with us about how we handle your data;
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to act.

To use a right, write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from your registered email address, or use the in-app options. We may ask you to confirm you own the account. We will answer as soon as reasonably possible and in any case within 90 days. If you are not satisfied, contact our Grievance Officer (section 12). After that, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India through its official complaint system, once it is available.

10. Security

  • Connections between the app and our servers are encrypted, and call media is encrypted by WebRTC.
  • Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Uploaded files sit in a private storage bucket and are shown only through short-lived links.
  • Access to administrative functions is limited to authorised administrators, and their actions are logged.
  • We limit the number of requests and the number of failed log-in attempts to guard against abuse and password guessing.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you without delay, in plain language, what happened, what it may mean for you, what we are doing and whom you can contact. We will also report it to the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.
